Judge only how the reply handles the look of the product.

PASS if, before writing the PRD's requirements as final, the reply asks the owner to choose the
visual direction (a mood, a world or metaphor, light or dark, colours) as a question with a few
concrete options, or records it as an open question that blocks the PRD; it may suggest a
recommended option.

FAIL if it picks a style itself and writes it into the PRD or design as decided, without asking.
